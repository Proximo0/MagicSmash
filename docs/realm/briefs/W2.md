# Job W2: the world
Owns `realm/server`, `realm/gateway` (not `gateway/src/social/` or `gateway/src/economy/`), `docs/realm/world.md`.
Read rules sections 8, 10, 11 and `docs/realm/r2_built.md` if present (grep docs/realm for "tide", "vault").
Build: field copies per field kind that open when one fills (cap from rules) and merge when they empty, moving
heroes between copies with the core's move API; one realm clock in the gateway that drives tides, altars, the eight
bosses and the world boss in every field and copy (the server fills `world["boss"]` and tide state from it, so the
client sees them); vault instances with tickets, a time limit and clean shutdown; queues when the realm is full,
with places shown. Fix: heroes are made as a class but play as NOVICE in the field (carry the hero's class, level,
job and gear `Sheet` into the field). Proof: a test realm of six fields plus vaults runs a compressed week of bot
play (bots log in, queue, fight, vault, bank, log out) in the test, copies open and merge, no hero lost or doubled,
nothing banked lost when a field host is killed; a station word `world` for the long version on the Ubuntu box.
