exposed calls, and the `frames` station job. Rules: rules.md sections 1-8 as they show on screen.

laptop and heatherspc. Proof: `realm/check_client.sh` passes; screenshots at zoom 1/2/4 written to a dir you name in
the report; frame numbers in software rendering. Doc: `docs/realm/client.md`.
