# Job B1: bots that play like people, and balance
Owns `realm/loadtest` (bots), `docs/realm/balance.md`, and number tables in `realm/core` and `realm/loot` data
(numbers only; no rule changes without saying so in the report). Read rules section 2 (curve targets) and 13
(gates), `docs/realm/economy.md`.
Build: bots that play careers 1 to 60 like people (choose skills and jobs, use potions, bank, party up, die
sometimes), every class and job, alone and in parties up to eight. Run them and tune the tables until: the curve
targets of section 2 hold for every class (no class more than 15% behind the median at any level band); the
party gate on fields 0-2 and the jobs gate of section 13 (unmet after R2) pass; the economy coin sink removes at
least what monsters mint over a career (add sinks in data: repair, travel, smith costs). Proof: `balance.md` with
tables per class and level band, a test that runs a short version of each gate, a station word `balance` for the
full careers on a station.
