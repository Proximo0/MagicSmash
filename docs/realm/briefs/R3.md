# Job R3: players against players (the Blood Mark, altar wars, duels, marked realms)
Owns `realm/core` and `realm/gateway/src/social`. Rules: rules.md section 14 only (plus section 6's level ceiling
and section 7's FALLEN rule where 14 points at them), `docs/realm/rules_built.md`, `docs/realm/social.md` (guilds),
`docs/realm/wire.md` (tag ranges: take new tags from the social range in `realm/gateway/src/proto/ranges.rs`, never
reuse a number). `marked` and the last-struck tick are already in the outing state and the hash: find them with
`grep -n` and build on them; no saved format may change.

Build, for the whole game (all six fields, every class and skill):
1. **The Blood Mark** in `realm/core`: taking the mark (class level 20+, hold interact 5 s at a waystone), marked
   heroes' attacks and skills striking other marked heroes at 40% of monster damage, the heal/shield/raise ban for
   unmarked helpers of a marked hero who struck a hero in the last 60 s, +25% coin and +15 luck while marked, the
   haul loss of a hero downed by a hero's blow going to the hero who downed her, the ten-minute wait before the mark
   can be put down (kept across leaving the field), safe ground (300 px of a waystone or the gate; town), the level
   ceiling. Events for every new outcome (section 12 order kept). Hero-on-hero hit tests must reuse the spatial grid
   so the tick stays inside the budget.
2. **Guild altar wars**: two guilds declare for a field's tide (gateway/social: declare, accept, list, cancel,
   officer rank only, wire messages and store through the existing guild code); in core their members are marked
   against each other only, for that tide; held altar time is counted per guild and a result event names the winner.
3. **Duels** in the town's training yard: challenge, accept, decline, a duel instance in core with both heroes
   marked against each other only, no haul loss, ends at downed or 3 minutes; result to both.
4. **Marked realms**: a realm flag that marks every hero past class level 20 for good under the same rules.
5. Marked bots in `realm/core/src/bots` (or loadtest if bots live there) that hunt each other, for the proof.

Proof (new tests in `realm/core/tests/` and the gateway's tests):
- 1,000 heroes with half of them marked bots fighting each other among 20,000 monsters: tick mean and p99 inside
  the 50 ms budget on 4 cores; print the bench line.
- Property test over many seeds: no unmarked hero ever takes damage from a hero or loses haul to one; nobody is
  struck on safe ground; a duel or altar war never lets a third party be struck.
- Same-hash test still equal across thread counts; `./check.sh` prints `REALM ok ...`.
Doc: `docs/realm/pvp.md` with every number chosen and why, and open questions; add an R3 note to `rules_built.md`.
