# Job H1: hearth (homes and social places)
Owns `realm/gateway/src/homes/` (new module), `realm/client/homes/` (new), `docs/realm/homes.md`, and only the
gateway/proto/client hooks it needs (small additions; new wire tags in an unused range, check proto.rs first: taken
are 0x30s, 0x40..0x5B, 0x8B, 0x90, 0xA8, 0xB0). Read the plan's houses section in `docs/realm/rules.md` (grep
"house", "hearth", "plaza") and `docs/realm/social.md`, `docs/realm/economy.md`.
Build: streets of houses (a street holds 24 plots; a hero's account buys or rents one with coin through the store
ledger), house rooms with furniture placed on a grid (furniture and trophies are items from the store: placing
moves the item into the house, never copies), visiting rules (open, friends, guild, locked), a guestbook; the
shared places: plaza, tavern, market square (E1's market stalls as a place), guild hall (T1's guild), training
yard (a dummy that reports damage per second), campfires (sit for a rest buff). Town rooms carry players between
these places. Client: a house screen with a placement grid, the street map, the places as town rooms, all drawn by
code in the dark frame style with flat stand-ins named for the art list (`Art`). Proof: 24 houses on a street
visited by 200 bots (wire test), no item copied or lost by placing and picking up (store verify), and the client's
shell test walks into a house and places a chair.
